# Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative community where all contributors and users feel safe and valued. This Code of Conduct outlines the standards we expect from everyone who participates in this project.


## Expected Conduct

All community members are expected to:
- Engage respectfully in all interactions
- Use clear, inclusive language that respects diverse perspectives
- Actively listen to and respect different viewpoints
- Report concerns through appropriate channels rather than public shaming

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behaviors are not tolerated:
- Harassment, intimidation, or bullying of any kind
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on identity, background, or beliefs
- Threats, violence, or abusive language
- Deliberate doxxing or sharing of personal information without consent
- Posting or attempting to share sensitive data, credentials, production secrets, or confidential information
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or project activities
- Any form of coercion or manipulation

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct, contact maintainers immediately at  sacro.contact@uwe.ac.uk to enable rapid mitigation


## Enforcement

Maintainers will:
- Investigate all reports fairly and promptly
- Take appropriate action, including:
  - Issuing warnings or requests for corrective behavior
  - Removing, editing, or redacting violating content
  - Temporarily or permanently restricting access to the project
- In cases of data or security risk, take immediate mitigation measures (e.g., revoking access, resetting credentials)
- Maintain confidentiality throughout the process while keeping relevant parties informed

## Acknowledgment

Thank you for helping us build a welcoming, safe, and productive community. We appreciate your commitment to these values.
