# Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative community where all contributors and users feel safe and valued. This Code of Conduct outlines the standards we expect from everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected Conduct

All community members are expected to engage respectfully in their interactions with others. We encourage the use of clear and inclusive language, the respectful exchange of ideas, and constructive feedback that helps improve the project and community. Contributors should actively listen to differing viewpoints, approach discussions in good faith, and raise concerns through appropriate channels rather than through public shaming or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, intimidation, bullying, discrimination, threats, abusive language, or any behaviour that creates a hostile environment will not be tolerated. This includes sharing personal information without consent, attempting to expose sensitive data or credentials, deliberately disrupting project activities, engaging in inflammatory or malicious behaviour, or retaliating against individuals who report concerns or participate in investigations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, discussions, pull requests, project documentation, communication channels, community meetings, workshops, and events. It also applies when an individual is publicly representing the project or its community.

## Reporting Violations

Anyone who witnesses or experiences behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct is encouraged to contact the maintainers at <sacro.contact@uwe.ac.uk>. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They will take reasonable and appropriate action in response to behaviour that violates these standards and will strive to ensure that all reports are handled fairly, consistently, and respectfully.

## Enforcement

When a violation occurs, maintainers may take actions including:

- Providing guidance or requesting corrective behaviour.
- Issuing a formal warning.
- Temporarily restricting participation in project activities.
- Permanently removing an individual from the community.

Maintainers may also remove or edit content that violates this Code of Conduct, revoke access to project resources where necessary, and take immediate action in situations involving security, privacy, or data protection risks. All reports will be investigated fairly and promptly, and maintainers will endeavour to maintain confidentiality throughout the process while keeping relevant parties informed where appropriate.

## Acknowledgment

Thank you for helping us build a welcoming, safe, and productive community. We appreciate your commitment to these values and your contribution to a positive environment for all participants.
